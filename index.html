Welcome!

I am a master's student in Artificial Intelligence (AIM) at Carnegie Mellon University (U.S. News #1 in AI). My work focuses on AI systems and networked intelligence, especially secure edge networks, LLM-inference-enabled service systems, computer vision, and data-driven agentic workflows.

I am currently an algorithm intern at KargoBot in the CTO-Autonomy-E2E team, working on retrieval, provenance, and agentic evidence analysis. I enjoy building systems that connect rigorous problem formulation with practical implementation.

Selected Projects

Research Area 1: Edge AI Agent Deployment and Network Security Optimization

NFV Security-aware Small-Scale Inference Agents Deployment

Jun 2025 - May 2026

First-authored work on security-constrained, cost-aware deployment of small-scale inference agents in heterogeneous edge environments. Formulated the NFV-enabled Cost-Security Balanced Deployment (NCBD) model and proposed CS-ND with a Cost-Security Efficiency Index (CSEI) for selecting security network functions (S-NFs). Validated with 1,843,200 simulations against Cost-first, Price-first, and Security-first baselines, achieving up to 59.5% cost reduction versus Security-first and 50.1% improvement over Cost-first. Independently completed problem formulation, modeling, algorithm design, simulation, and writing. Published in Computer Networks (CCF-B), 2026.

code / paper

Asymmetric Protection for LLM-Inference-enabled Networks

Oct 2024 - Jul 2025

First-authored work proposing asymmetric protection so that primary and backup S-SFCs can differ in function order, version, and type while satisfying SeLA security constraints and fault-disjointness. Formulated the S-SFCEP problem, proved NP-hardness, and designed NS-SD with a Cost-Security Optimization (CSO) metric. On the NY-20 topology, reduced total resource cost by up to 56% versus mainstream methods while shortening runtime. Independently completed modeling, complexity analysis, algorithm design, evaluation, and writing. Published at ICCC 2025 (EI), Best Student Paper Award.

code / paper

Research Area 2: Diffusion Models and Intelligent Vision Applications

Image Rotation Correction via Diffusion and Consistency Models

Apr 2024 - May 2025

First-authored work on image rotation correction in complex natural scenes. Combined consistency models with discrete wavelet transforms and implemented DDPM, DDIM, and Stable Diffusion baselines in PyTorch. Evaluated on an ImageNet subset, achieving PSNR 21.6 and SSIM 0.67, outperforming traditional rotation cropping and optical-flow prediction. Independently led method design, model implementation, evaluation, and writing. Published at ICAID 2025.
